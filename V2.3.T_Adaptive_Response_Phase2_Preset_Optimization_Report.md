## Search policy and evidence

Each preset received 40 deterministic joint primary samples across horizon,
maximum lead, turning protection, and turning margin. Up to four Pareto parents
then received bounded local refinement of velocity response, acceleration
response, horizon extension, extension cap, reversal response, and deceleration
response. Onset, sustained, settling, noise-rejection, and motion-sensitivity
values remained fixed from the corresponding baseline.

This produced 285 candidates. All 285 passed finite-output, legal-range,
persistent-stationary-drift, Pattern-73, stop, and reversal hard gates. The
candidate score keeps separately normalized responsiveness, future accuracy,
reversal, stop, stability, noise, precision, and turning components. The fixed
per-preset weights, complete vectors, raw component metrics, rejection file,
and Pareto fronts are in the accompanying artifact directory.

Independent validation occurred only after finalist selection. Real-human
traces were only a generalization and comparison gate; they were never used as
search fitness.

## Robustness, physical evidence, and cost

Small horizon and lead perturbations are retained in robustness_analysis.csv;
none crossed the Pattern-73 gate. Per-trace physical metrics are retained
without averaging away outliers in physical_finalist_results.csv.

The synthetic hot-path benchmark uses the frozen source only and makes no
DirectInput or vJoy call. Its all-eight-axis result was 3.2 us typical, 5.1 us
p95, 6.3 us p99, and zero allocations, or about 0.158 percent of a 4 ms
250 Hz period. Parameter values do not alter the mapper structural allocation
or locking behavior. This is local synthetic timing, not physical-device
latency evidence.
