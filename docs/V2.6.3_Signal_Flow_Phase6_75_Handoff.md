## Accepted handoff state

- Graph cards retain their compact at-rest presentation; hovering a card
  reveals its port sections for direct work.
- While a source wire is being dragged, the source card remains expanded even
  as the pointer opens a prospective destination card.
- A card drop promotes its final presentation position immediately. It does
  not mutate canonical topology, recreate routes, rebuild the whole wire
  cache, or replay connection-creation animation.
- Incident wires retain the final live-drag curve and smoothly morph into the
  newly settled, obstacle-aware geometry at release. Unrelated wires remain
  stable unless the moved card has genuinely changed their obstacle path.
- Workspace persistence and its backend acknowledgement remain visually
  silent. A saved position matches the final presentation position; there is
  no delayed second snap.
