### Pattern-73 classifier correction and residual

The test-only classifier correction was applied identically to both A/B
harnesses.  It preserves each raw reversal event, and reclassifies an event as
`HARNESS` only when a human intent is conveyed, the stale lead is cancelled
within one mapper/source period plus 0.5 ms, and wrong-direction lead area is
at most `0.0001`.  Nine Pattern-73 rows are therefore correctly `HARNESS`.

Alpha-Beta is intentionally not reclassified: the candidate has a predictor
miss at human intent 300 ms / observed reversal 308 ms, immediate stale-lead
cancellation (0.000119209 ms), but wrong-direction lead area `0.000121035`,
above the threshold.  The frozen baseline's identical event is `0.0000709266`
with peak stale lead `0.0177316`; candidate peak stale lead is `0.0302587`.
The candidate consequently retains one reversal-timing and one false-reversal
product-suspect row.  This is a product-facing new-math regression, not a
classifier defect.

The other candidate product-suspect records are Velocity on
`persona/space-sim-0`, `persona/combat-helicopter-3`, and
`persona/combat-helicopter-1`, plus Preset Extreme on `persona/space-sim-0`.
Each has a preserved event row in `candidate-canonical\reversal_events.csv`.
The first, second, and fourth had a baseline detection where candidate now
reports a miss; the helicopter-1 miss is present in both snapshots and remains
an unresolved product-facing record.  The six Pattern-72 rows remain
`UNKNOWN`; they were not reclassified speculatively.

## Physical-trace and new-mechanism evidence

All five current presets were replayed over all 15 recorded traces.  The
per-trace deltas are in `physical_ab_per_trace.csv`; the aggregate scorecard is
in `preset_scorecard.csv`.  Candidate mean absolute error increased slightly
for every preset (Light +0.000196, Balanced +0.000581, Fast +0.000874,
Aggressive +0.001021, Extreme +0.001030).  Mean peak error nevertheless fell
for all five, while wrong-direction area rose for all five (Balanced +0.000119,
Extreme +0.000486).  No aggregate physical-trace result may be read as a
license to dismiss the Pattern-73 and persona reversal records above.

The new test-only authority telemetry demonstrates that the new mechanisms are
actually active rather than configured inertly.  Across the standard runs,
Balanced had mean/peak onset authority 0.001843/0.010440, mean sustained
authority 0.001367, and mean/peak extension 0.000400/0.526847 ms; Extreme had
0.025442/0.142500 onset authority, 0.009626 sustained authority, and
0.005389/5.110649 ms extension.  Turning confidence reached 0.952497.  The
35-configuration horizon matrix covers base horizons 0, 4, 12, 20, and 30 ms
against caps 0, 4, 8, 12, 18, 24, and 30 ms; the enabled cases show monotonic
nonzero extension.  See `authority_metrics_by_preset.csv` and
`horizon_extension_metrics.csv` for the complete raw metrics.
